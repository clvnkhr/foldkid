# Tarot artwork

Original Rider–Waite–Smith illustrations by Pamela Colman Smith, published 1910.

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, [TaionWC scan collection](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC)). Commons identifies these files as public domain.

Images are resized to at most 360px wide and 640px tall, retaining their original proportions, and encoded as WebP (quality 35, method 6) for slow connections. The artwork is otherwise unaltered. Individual file pages and licenses are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).
