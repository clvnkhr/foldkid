# IPA Audio Attribution

These recordings are used by Phoneme Garden and were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons IPA audio file pages. The app loads local AAC `.m4a` conversions for mobile/Safari compatibility; the original `.ogg` downloads are kept beside them as source assets.

Most vowel files in this starter set are attributed on Commons to Denelson83 and are available under GFDL 1.2-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0. The consonant files are Wikimedia Commons IPA recordings with license and author details on their file pages.

- `close-back-rounded-vowel.ogg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_back_rounded_vowel.ogg
- `close-front-unrounded-vowel.ogg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Close_front_unrounded_vowel.ogg
- `open-front-unrounded-vowel.ogg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Open_front_unrounded_vowel.ogg
- `open-mid-back-rounded-vowel.ogg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Open-mid_back_rounded_vowel.ogg

- `velar-nasal.ogg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Velar_nasal.ogg
